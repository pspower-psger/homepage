// What We Do

Core Services

End-to-end power systems engineering across planning, design, analysis, and research domains.

01
Transmission Planning & Analysis
Load flow, contingency, and stability studies for bulk power transmission systems. Long-term planning studies aligned with reliability standards.
02
Renewable Energy Integration
Grid interconnection studies for solar, wind, and storage projects. Inverter-based resource modeling, harmonic analysis, and dynamic assessment.
03
Power System Stability Studies
Transient, voltage, and small-signal stability analysis. Dynamic simulations using industry-standard tools (PSS/E, PowerWorld, PSCAD).
04
Protection & Control Engineering
Relay coordination, protection scheme design, and settings calculations. SCADA/EMS integration and substation automation consulting.
05
Grid Resilience & Reliability
N-1 and N-2 contingency analysis, critical infrastructure vulnerability assessment, and resilience improvement planning for utilities and ISOs.
06
Applied Power Systems Research
Original research in grid modernization, energy storage, HVDC, and advanced power electronics. Technical publications and white papers.

// About PSGER

Built for the Modern Grid

PSGER was founded by power engineers with a conviction that rigorous analysis and innovative research must go hand in hand. We serve utilities, independent power producers, developers, and regulatory bodies navigating the complexity of the evolving grid.

Our multidisciplinary team combines deep expertise in classical power systems with cutting-edge computational methods — bringing academic rigor to real-world engineering challenges.
