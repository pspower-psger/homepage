■ About PSGER

PSGER (Power Systems Grid Engineering & Research) is a specialized technical organization engaged in engineering consultancy and applied research in the field of electric power systems. Founded with the objective of bridging the gap between academic research and practical grid engineering, PSGER serves utilities, independent power producers, grid operators, and government bodies.

Our team of qualified power engineers brings expertise across transmission planning, system stability, protection engineering, and advanced grid studies. We adhere to IEEE, IEC, and CEA standards in all our engagements.
